Pinpop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Pinpop is a location-sharing app for friends. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it. We do not run ads, do not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and do not sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Information we collect

  • Account & profile — email or Apple/Google account identifier used to sign in, display name, username, and profile photo.
  • Location — your device's GPS location, shared with your friends according to the sharing level you choose (see below).
  • Messages & social activity — chat messages, waves, reactions, and "throws" you send to friends; friend requests and streaks.
  • Highlights — photos and captions you choose to post, along with the location you posted them from.
  • Places — check-ins at nearby places, and Zenlands (custom locations you create and label for friends).
  • Status & device signals — the status text/emoji you set, your battery level, and a push-notification token so we can deliver alerts.

We only collect what powers a feature you're actively using — there is no background data collection beyond your live location while you have sharing turned on.

Location sharing is yours to control

Every account has a sharing mode, changeable any time in Settings:

  • Precise — friends see your exact live location.
  • Blurred — friends see a randomly offset location (roughly 0.2–1.2 km away from you); your real coordinates never leave our servers.
  • Frozen — friends see your last shared location, frozen in place; live updates stop entirely.

Blurring and freezing both happen on our server, not on your device — a friend's app is never sent your precise coordinates unless you've chosen Precise. You can also override this per friend.

How we use your information

  • To show your location to friends per your chosen sharing mode, and show you theirs.
  • To operate chat, waves, reactions, streaks, Highlights, check-ins, and Zenlands.
  • To send push notifications for things like friend requests, messages, and waves.
  • To keep the service secure and working (e.g. rate-limiting abuse, debugging crashes you report to us directly).

We do not use your data for advertising, do not build advertising or analytics profiles, and do not use it to train third-party AI models.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, our backend hosting provider, stores your data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Apple or Google, only if you choose to sign in with them — they handle authentication and share only the name/email you approve.
  • Apple Push Notification service, to deliver notifications to your device.
  • OpenStreetMap, to load map tiles — your device requests these directly, so your IP address is visible to OpenStreetMap while a tile loads, the same as loading any map on the web.

We never sell your data, and never share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices

  • Change your sharing mode, per-friend overrides, or username/profile at any time in Settings.
  • Unfriend, block, or leave a group chat at any time.
  • Delete individual messages, Highlights, or check-ins from within the app.
  • Delete your entire account from Settings → Account → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent: your profile, location history, messages, Highlights, and check-ins are erased from our servers right away, with nothing kept in cold storage afterward.

Data retention

We keep your data only as long as your account is active, so the app can keep working the way you'd expect (showing your friends' recent activity, streaks, and history with them). Deleting your account removes it immediately, as described above.

Children

Pinpop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
